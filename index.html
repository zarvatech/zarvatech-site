FAQ

Questions clients ask first

Do you work with clients outside India?+

Yes. We work remotely with teams in India and abroad, and plan calls around your time zone.

How do you price a project?+

After a free call we send a written scope with a timeline and quote. Ongoing work can be billed monthly.

Can you take over an existing codebase?+

Yes. We start with a short review of the code and infrastructure, then fix, extend or modernize it.

Can AI run on our own servers?+

Yes. We have built AI assistants that run entirely on-premise, on CPU-only machines with no internet access.

What happens after launch?+

We stay available for fixes, monitoring and improvements, either as needed or on a monthly arrangement.

Have a problem worth solving?

Tell us what you are trying to fix. We will reply with how we would approach it and what it would take.
